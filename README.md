## About

R&D Engineer at **Musashi Seimitsu Industry Co., Ltd.** (Japan) working on automotive electro-mechanical systems. My work involves developing embedded control systems for actuators and sensors, where mechanical behavior, electrical signals, and firmware must work together reliably.

I handle the full development cycle: understanding mechanical constraints, designing signal conditioning circuits, writing firmware, and validating the integrated system.
